Tharaka Wijethunge

Electrical, Computer & Systems Engineering
Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute

I develop optimization and control methods for resource allocation in networked systems. My work combines mathematical modeling and machine learning, with applications in building energy, AI inference, and communication networks.

I am interested in how local decisions affect the performance, efficiency, and reliability of a larger system.
